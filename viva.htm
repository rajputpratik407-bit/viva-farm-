Our Story

A Sanctuary of elegance & Nature

Tucked away in the countryside, Veva Farm began as a family farmstead and blossomed into one of the region's most beloved wedding destinations.

Every celebration here is personal. Our dedicated planning team, in-house décor studio and trusted catering partners take care of every detail — from the first site visit to the final sparkler send-off — so you can simply be present.

Exchange vows beneath the century-old oak, dine under festoon lights in the garden pavilion, and dance the night away in our candlelit barn.

— The Veva Farm Family
